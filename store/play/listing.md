## Full description (4000 max)

Burns is a multiplayer card game for 2 to 8 players. Play pass-and-play with
friends and family on a single device, or take on computer opponents on your
own. The game works fully offline, has no ads, and has no in-app purchases.

HOW TO PLAY

Five shared rows are dealt face up in the middle of the table. Four Ace piles
build up by suit, from Ace to King. Every player gets their own face-down pile
and turns over one card at a time. You win by getting rid of all of your own
cards, including your discard pile.

There are three places a card can go:

- Ace piles, building up by suit from Ace to King.
- The five shared rows, building down by one rank in alternating colors. A card
  brings the run beneath it along when it moves, and any card can claim an empty
  row.
- Your opponents' discard piles, building up or down by one rank in alternating
  colors. Their pile is somewhere to unload your cards, and somewhere they can
  unload theirs on you.

CALLING BURNS

If a play was available to you and you did not make it, any other player can
call Burns on you when your turn ends. A correct call means the player who
missed the play takes one card from everyone else at the table. An incorrect
call means the caller takes those cards instead. Plays also follow a set
priority - Ace piles first, then the shared rows, then opponents' discards - so
taking an easy play when a better one was available can be called too.

GAME MODES

- Pass and play: 2 to 8 players share one device, with a handoff screen between
  turns so no player sees another player's cards.
- Computer opponents: play alone against 1 to 7 computer players that follow the
  full rules and will call Burns when you miss a play.

FEATURES

- 2 to 8 players, in both game modes.
- Plays completely offline. No internet connection needed.
- No advertising of any kind.
- No in-app purchases.
- No account, sign-in, or email address required.
- Automatic save. Your table is saved after every move and can be resumed from
  the home screen.
- Built-in rules reference, readable at any point during a game.
- Hint button that shows an available play when you want one.
- Deck gallery: browse every card face, switch suits, and view the card back.
- Hold any face-up card to inspect its full artwork.
- Original hand-drawn card faces, court cards, and card back art.

DESIGNED FOR PHONES AND TABLETS

The whole board fits on screen at once. There is no scrolling, no pinch-to-zoom,
and no cards hidden off the edge. Portrait and landscape are both supported.
Long rows open a grid that shows every card in the run. The layout leaves room
for notches and gesture bars.

Drag a card to where it should go, or tap the card and then tap its destination.

Burns began as a family card game. These are that family's rules, edge cases
included, written down and settled.
