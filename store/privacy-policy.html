Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

Request data deletion

Pat Guettler (Grapegames) built Burns as a paid family card game for Google Play. Burns does not show ads, does not include an analytics SDK, and does not use in-app purchases. This page explains what information the app uses.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves game progress and preferences locally on your device (for example, an unfinished pass-and-play or computer game, and whether sound is on). This information stays on the device unless you remove it. It is not sent to Grapegames servers.

Optional Online Rooms

If you host or join a private online table, the app uses the Internet to talk to the room server you or a friend run. That server may see a display name, a room code, reconnect credentials, and the cards needed to run the game. Grapegames does not operate a public Burns server and does not collect this information on Grapegames servers. When you play only on one device, no network game data is created.

Third-Party Services

Google Play may process device and app data in order to distribute the app, check for updates, and handle the paid download. Burns does not include Google AdMob or other advertising SDKs.

Request Deletion of Your Data

  1. Game data on the device (saved games and sound setting): open your device Settings → Apps → Burns → Storage → Clear storage (wording may vary by device), or uninstall the app.
  2. Online room data: contact the operator of the room server you used to request deletion. Room state and reconnect keys are saved on that server and can survive a restart. Fully disconnected rooms expire after 24 hours of inactivity while the server is running. The server operator can remove saved room data and any backups sooner. Clearing your device does not delete the server’s copy.
  3. Contact me: email grapegames3141@gmail.com with the subject “Burns data deletion request” and I will respond with these steps or answer questions.

I do not operate user accounts; there is no separate account to delete on my servers.

Children’s Privacy

Burns is a family card game. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children on Grapegames servers. Local play data remains on the device as described above. Optional online rooms use only the names and cards needed to play at that table.

Security

If you use an online table, use a server you trust. Local game data on your device is protected by your device’s operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
